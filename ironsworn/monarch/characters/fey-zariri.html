Appearance

Fey is a young woman with pale brown skin and an athletic build. Her black hair is shaved on the sides as most voidborn, with the top kept long but braided and tied. Golden tattoos run down her limbs in fine, geometric lines, acting both as a distinguished symbols of her former clan and as a conduit for her abilities as a Marked. Fey keeps these covered with wraps or long sleeves when in public.

Fey often wears a spacer's jumpsuit (the form fitting underlayer beneath an environment suit) under an old cropped jacket with a high collar. She wears a sash cinched tight around her waist, into which is tucked her sword and her mask. Her knee-high boots are reinforced with metal at the shins.
