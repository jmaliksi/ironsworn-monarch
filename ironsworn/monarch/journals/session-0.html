Fey Zariri was a Founder clan Blade in training. From the moment her hands were strong enough, she was taught to hold iron. As all those born from the arks, she grew in a tube, but of the hundreds birthed in the same year, only 21 were Marked. 16 were trained as Blades. 5 were trained as Adepts.

The Marked, as their name implies, are born marked by the void: eyes that shimmer with gold and amethyst and black when under the effects of Dust, allowing them to see the connections that bind hearts and minds and planets and stars.

Of the 16 Blades in training, only 9 survived to see their final year at the academy. Fledglings were apprenticed as groups of 1–3 to a clan Blade, learning swordsmanship, politics, and everything needed to protect the people and clan assets. The final two years are spent doing field work, and this is where fledglings often fail. Fey was no exception; however, her failure was in running away. Her cohort had been sent to protect one of the many failure-to-be gravside colonies from forgespawn. Her Marked empathy, stronger than anticipated, was overwhelmed by the panic of evacuating colonists to the point where she was left on the ship. Had she fought alongside her cohort, they may not have all died. She was dishonorably discharged from the academy and exiled from the clan.

Had Fey not run away with the evacuees, she likely would have died with her master and her fellow trainees. Or perhaps she would have been the key needed to turn the tide.

Fey, now 23, has been working as an independent fighter aboard the Elara Five, providing escort to couriers and medical ships. She has transferred ships multiple times if ever the manifest brings them close to clan space, as she still stays far from her original clan's space as they have right to execute on sight.
